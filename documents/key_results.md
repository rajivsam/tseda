# Key Results

## Objective 1: Rapid, reproducible time series structural analysis

- KR 1.1: 90% of eligible datasets produce a completed decomposition and observation summary within 60 minutes of first loading.
- KR 1.2: At least 80% of users accept the default SSA window suggestion without manual tuning.
- KR 1.3: 100% of completed analyses generate an exportable summary and component dataframe.

## Objective 2: Reduce analyst risk and ambiguity in SSA decisions

- KR 2.1: SSA window selection is justified by sampling frequency and eigenvalue diagnostics in every run.
- KR 2.2: 0 false-positive change-point detections in baseline validation datasets where no regime shift exists.
- KR 2.3: AIC-based rank selection is applied automatically for all decompositions.

## Objective 3: Deliver actionable signal diagnostics for business workflows

- KR 3.1: Trend shift and seasonal amplitude shift detections are reported separately and visually distinct.
- KR 3.2: Noise diagnostic output includes both Durbin-Watson results and a plain-language independence assessment.
- KR 3.3: At least one export path (dataframe, plot, observation text) is available for every completed analysis.
