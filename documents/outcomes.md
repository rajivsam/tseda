# Outcomes

## Objective 1 outcomes

- Feature: Three-step guided workflow
  - Outcome: Analysts can follow a well-defined process with immediate diagnostic feedback.
- Feature: Automated data profiling and signal diagnostics
  - Outcome: Analysts spend less time on manual plot creation and more time on interpretation.
- Feature: Exportable results and observation logs
  - Outcome: Completed analyses are immediately shareable and reproducible.

## Objective 2 outcomes

- Feature: Sampling-frequency-based SSA window heuristic
  - Outcome: Default window settings reduce configuration risk for common series frequencies.
- Feature: AIC rank selection
  - Outcome: Model order is selected using a statistical criterion rather than subjective heuristics.
- Feature: Component grouping UI and reconstruction plots
  - Outcome: Analysts see exactly how trend, seasonality, and noise are split before accepting the model.

## Objective 3 outcomes

- Feature: Dual change-point detectors
  - Outcome: Trend and seasonal strength changes are distinguished and actionable.
- Feature: Noise diagnostics with Durbin-Watson and KDE
  - Outcome: Analysts receive clear guidance on whether residuals are suitable for further modeling.
- Feature: KMDS OWL observation export
  - Outcome: Analytical findings are preserved in a structured knowledge base for follow-up and audit.
