# Objectives

## Objective 1: Rapid, reproducible time series structural analysis

- Enable analysts to go from raw regularly sampled time series data to a validated decomposition and diagnosis in under one hour.
- Make the workflow repeatable and auditable with structured outputs and documented observations.

## Objective 2: Reduce analyst risk and ambiguity in SSA decisions

- Provide a principled SSA window selection and model rank decision process.
- Minimize manual tuning by capturing and surfacing key diagnostics automatically.

## Objective 3: Deliver actionable signal diagnostics for business workflows

- Surface trend shifts, seasonal strength changes, and noise independence clearly.
- Make decomposition outputs directly usable for forecasting, anomaly detection, and data-pipeline handoff.
